Privacy Policy for Presenza

 Presenza mobile application (package name: com.presenza). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

By using Presenza, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Presenza is an employee attendance and time-tracking application. To provide its core functionality, we collect the following types of data:

a) Personal Information

  • Name and Employee ID — to identify you within your organization.
  • Email address and phone number — for account creation, OTP-based login, and communication.
  • Organization/employer details — to associate your account with the correct workplace.

b) Attendance and Work Data

  • Check-in and check-out timestamps.
  • Break durations (coffee, lunch, other breaks).
  • Total working hours and attendance history.
  • Leave requests and approval status.

c) Authentication Data

  • One-Time Passwords (OTP) sent to your registered email/phone for secure login.
  • Device session tokens (stored securely, not shared).

d) Automatically Collected Data

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version (for diagnostics).
  • Crash logs and basic usage analytics (to improve app stability).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you via OTP and provide secure access.
  • To record and display your attendance, working hours, and breaks.
  • To process leave requests and show approval status.
  • To generate attendance reports and productivity analytics for you and your employer.
  • To send service-related notifications (e.g., shift reminders, leave approvals).
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your attendance and work data is shared only with:

  • Your employer/organization — as required for attendance management.
  • Service providers — such as cloud hosting and email/SMS delivery services, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal authorities — only when required by law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • OTP-based authentication reduces password-related risks.
  • Access to backend systems is restricted to authorized personnel only.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal and attendance data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services to your employer. When no longer required, data is deleted or anonymized, subject to legal retention requirements.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.

How to request data deletion: Send an email to paulfincap@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your registered email and employee ID. We will process your request within 30 days.

Note: Some attendance records may be retained if required by your employer's legal or payroll obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Presenza is intended for use by employees aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
